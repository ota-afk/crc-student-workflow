1. Does a student's arrival record or student status need time to appear in the systems the DMV checks before a visit? If so, how long, and how is it confirmed?

Possibly. Federal guidance allows time for record updates. The California ID sources reviewed have not confirmed a specific waiting period for ID cards.

- Older federal guidance — F-1 applicants for a driver's license or state ID: ICE's SEVP Fact Sheet 1103-02, updated September 5, 2012, says the SEVIS record must be Active. Page 2 instructs applicants to wait “at least ten calendar days” after U.S. entry and “at least two federal business days” after activation. Footnote 3 defines activation as the DSO's update from Initial to Active after the student reports to the school. This is dated federal guidance, not a newly confirmed California intake rule. [ICE SEVP fact sheet, page 2 and footnote 3](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/pdf/dmv_factsheet.pdf).

- Official information — California legal-presence verification: DMV describes electronic verification with USCIS and secondary verification when the electronic check is unsuccessful. This page does not specify the intervals, a day-zero convention or a guaranteed processing time. It does not resolve whether California currently enforces those intervals at intake. [DMV legal-presence verification](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/limited-term-for-legal-presence/).

- Unconfirmed: CRC's activation turnaround and notification method are not established by the reviewed pages. ICE directs students to their DSO before applying. [ICE SEVP fact sheet](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/pdf/dmv_factsheet.pdf).

- Proposed App behavior: Keep the DSO activation date separate from check-in and any later email. If activation or a required date is unknown, show it as unknown instead of calculating a definite date. If adopted, count the entry/activation date as day zero, add 10 calendar days after entry, and count two subsequent weekdays after activation, excluding OPM observed federal holidays. This is an App convention; OPM holidays do not set DMV office hours. A calculated date cannot guarantee DMV verification. [OPM Federal Holidays](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/).

3. Which documents does the DMV list for an F-1 student, including a student without an SSN?

Use the published document list for the selected ID type and distinguish SSN ineligibility from an SSN application that is pending.

- Identity: DMV's Standard list includes a valid foreign passport with an approved I-94 as an identity option for applicants with satisfactory proof of legal presence. The noncitizen REAL ID list includes an unexpired foreign passport with a valid U.S. visa and approved I-94; a valid EAD is another listed option. [DMV Standard list, page 1](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/federal-non-compliant-dl-id-card-documents-list-pdf/), [DMV noncitizen REAL ID guidance](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/real-id-info-non-u-s-citizens/).

- Residency: Standard ID requires one acceptable residency document; REAL ID requires two different printed documents. Examples include signed leases, utility bills, bank records and qualifying school documents. The REAL ID checklist does not say they must come from different institutions. Identity evidence has original/certified-copy requirements, while permitted copies can be used for residency evidence. Follow the published name, address and exception instructions. [DMV Standard list, page 2](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/federal-non-compliant-dl-id-card-documents-list-pdf/), [REAL ID checklist](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/real-id-checklist/), [DMV noncitizen guidance](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/real-id-info-non-u-s-citizens/).

- SSN: DMV's FFDL 8 says legally present applicants who are ineligible for an SSN are exempt from the SSN requirement and may start an ID application, subject to verification. Eligible applicants must provide their number; the publication says the physical SSN card is not required for an ordinary noncommercial DL/ID application. It does not give the exact online checkbox wording or establish a universal SSA-L676 requirement. Those details are not specified here. [DMV FFDL 8, revision January 2020](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/educational-materials/fast-facts/social-security-number-ffdl-8/).

- I-20: SEVP's October 12, 2021 guidance permits DSO electronic signatures and transmission, and says students should print the received copy. DHS instructs both student and DSO to sign; the official sample shows the student signature/date field on page 1. Print the DSO-issued PDF and complete the Student Attestation; follow parent/guardian signature instructions if under 18. This establishes federal issuance/signing instructions, not a separate California DMV acceptance guarantee or wet-ink-only rule. [SEVP electronic I-20 guidance, page 2](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/pdf/I20-guidance.pdf), [DHS Form I-20 student guidance](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/prepare/students-and-the-form-i-20), [official sample I-20, page 1](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/assets/sample_formi20.pdf), [DMV noncitizen REAL ID guidance](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/real-id-info-non-u-s-citizens/).

4. DMV fee, hours, appointment rules, phone, and the official page for a first-time ID or REAL ID.

The regular original ID fee is $40, and the application is completed at a DMV field office.

- Fee and process: DMV lists $40 for a regular ID and $11 for a qualifying reduced-fee ID. First-time applicants complete the DL/ID application, provide the required evidence and applicable SSN information, pay the fee, and have a thumbprint scan and photo taken. Online preparation is available, followed by an office visit. [DMV licensing fees](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/licensing-fees/), [DMV ID instructions](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/identification-id-cards/), [online application](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/dl-id-online-app-edl-44/), [REAL ID application page](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/).

- Appointments: DMV's REAL ID FAQ directs applicants to make an appointment or check local office wait times. Use the current appointment/application flow for the relevant service. No fixed walk-in cutoff or average wait is established by these sources. [DMV REAL ID FAQ](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/), [DMV appointments](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/appointments/).

- Office: Sacramento South DMV is at 7775 La Mancha Way, Sacramento, CA 95823. The displayed schedule shows ordinary weekday hours of 8 a.m.–5 p.m., except Wednesday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., with weekends closed and holiday exceptions. DMV customer service is 1-800-777-0133, Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Check the office's dated schedule before a visit. [Sacramento South office](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/field-office/sacramento-south/), [DMV contact information](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/contacting-dmv/).

5. The exact steps, hours, location and contact for international student check-in.

CRC publishes its reporting instruction and enrollment steps, while referring admitted students to their welcome packet for arrival information.

- Reporting: Students should report to the International Student Advisor as soon as they arrive and keep their residential address current through Admissions and Records. This is the published arrival instruction; it is not a complete arrival-document checklist. [CRC International Students Office](https://crc.losrios.edu/student-resources/support-services/international-students-office).

- Federal address rule — F-1 students: The I-20 Notice of Address says to report a U.S. address to the DSO on arrival and report a new address within 10 days after a move; the DSO updates SEVIS. The deadline is triggered by an address change. This passage does not establish a blanket AR-11 filing requirement. [Official sample I-20, page 3](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/assets/sample_formi20.pdf).

- Enrollment: The published sequence is guided self-placement where applicable, meeting a counselor, enrolling in classes, paying fees and completing international orientation. CRC generally requires 12 units, with approved exceptions described on the international office page. Orientation is mandatory before the semester, and the date and time are emailed to students. [CRC enrollment steps](https://crc.losrios.edu/admissions/get-started-and-apply/international-student-enrollment-steps), [CRC International Students Office](https://crc.losrios.edu/student-resources/support-services/international-students-office).

- Arrival instructions: The admissions page directs students to the welcome packet sent with their admission letter. It also lists application documents, PDF submission by email and health insurance as a pre-enrollment requirement. Those application instructions do not establish that arrival check-in uses the same submission method or requires the same originals. A complete public arrival checklist, remote-check-in procedure and automatic completion notice are not established by these pages. [CRC admission steps](https://crc.losrios.edu/admissions/get-started-and-apply/international-student-admission-steps).

- Contact: CRC-InternationalStudents@crc.losrios.edu; (916) 691-7411. CRC lists in-person or phone appointments through this number. Admissions and Records is in College Center, CC 151, at the main campus, 8401 Center Parkway, Sacramento, CA 95823. Its published Fall hours for August 3–December 22, 2026 are Monday–Thursday 8 a.m.–5 p.m. and Friday 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m., subject to closures. These are A&R hours, not independently established advisor check-in hours. [CRC admission steps](https://crc.losrios.edu/admissions/get-started-and-apply/international-student-admission-steps), [CRC Admissions and Records](https://crc.losrios.edu/admissions/admissions-and-records-office).
