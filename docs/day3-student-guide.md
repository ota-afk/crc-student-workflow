## Apply for a California REAL ID card

### Prepare your documents

Everyone applying should prepare:

- Two different printed California address documents, such as a lease signed by you and the landlord, a utility/mobile bill, or a bank record. They must show your first and last names and the address on your application; the checklist explains exceptions.

Depending on your situation, you may also need:

- **If you are using your foreign passport as proof of identity:** your unexpired passport and valid U.S. visa, with an approved I-94. Follow DMV’s original/certified-copy instructions for identity documents.
- **If your current name differs from your identity document:** name-change documents.

Sources: California DMV’s [non-U.S. citizen document list](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/real-id-info-non-u-s-citizens/) and [REAL ID checklist](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/real-id-checklist/). The document list also includes other accepted identity options.

You can retrieve your latest electronic I-94 from CBP and print it if needed. [Official CBP I-94 service](https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/) · [USAGov I-94 instructions](https://www.usa.gov/arrival-departure-record)

### Apply for an SSN or check an application

- **Call Social Security (SSA).** Call **1-800-772-1213** on weekdays, 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. local time in California. The TTY number is **1-800-325-0778**. Ask for appointment help or ask about an application you already filed. [SSA phone support](https://www.ssa.gov/agency/contact/phone.html)
- **Start your first application online.** Use the first-time SSN page, then follow SSA’s instructions for showing your documents at an office. [First-time SSN application](https://www.ssa.gov/number-card/request-number-first-time)
- **Book online.** Select **Get started** on SSA’s appointment page and answer the questions about your visit. You can also call the number above for scheduling help. [SSA appointment page](https://www.ssa.gov/manage-benefits/make-an-appointment)
- **Find an office or Card Center.** Enter your ZIP code to see the address, local phone number and services. [SSA office locator](https://www.ssa.gov/locator)

**What to say if you’re applying for the first time**

> Hi, I’m an F-1 student applying for a Social Security number for the first time. Can you tell me what documents I need and help me schedule a visit if I need one?

**What to say if you already applied**

> I applied for an SSN on [date] and I’m still waiting. Can you check whether you need any more documents from me and tell me what I should do next?

### Ask DMV about REAL ID without an SSN

DMV describes an exemption for legally present applicants who are ineligible for an SSN. If you already have a number, provide it to DMV. If your application for an SSN is pending, tell DMV that you’re still waiting so they can explain your next step. [DMV SSN guidance](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/educational-materials/fast-facts/social-security-number-ffdl-8/)

- **Call or chat with DMV.** Call **1-800-777-0133** on weekdays, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The TTY number is **1-800-368-4327**. For online help, choose **Chat with a Live Agent**. [DMV customer service](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/contacting-dmv/)
- **Book your visit.** Open the appointment page and choose **First-time REAL ID Application**. If the option is unavailable or your case is unclear, call DMV for help choosing a service. [DMV appointments](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/appointments/)
- **Find a DMV field office.** Search by ZIP code or city for its address and check the office calendar before leaving. [DMV office finder](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/locations/)

**What to say to DMV**

> I’m applying for my first California REAL ID card and I don’t have an SSN. Can you tell me whether I can apply in my situation, which documents to bring, and how to book the right appointment?

If your SSN application is pending, add this question:

> My SSN application is still pending. Can I start the REAL ID application now, or should I wait until I receive the number?

### Plan your visit

**Fee:** $40 for a regular original ID card. Separate reduced/no-fee options have their own eligibility rules. [DMV fees](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/licensing-fees/).

**Application:** You can start online; DMV also describes a paper form available at its offices. Your first REAL ID requires an in-person visit. Use DMV’s visit information to check local wait times. [Application options](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/dl-id-online-app-edl-44/) · [REAL ID visit information](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/).

**Official recommendation:** use DMV’s full checklist to prepare your documents before visiting. [DMV REAL ID FAQ](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/what-is-real-id/).
