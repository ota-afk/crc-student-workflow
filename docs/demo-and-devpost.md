# Demo walkthrough and Devpost copy

## Recording sequence

Start a fresh session in the [deployed dashboard](https://crc-workflow-dashboard.onrender.com/) with the built-in fictional CRC profile. Follow the [pre-recording checklist](release-checklist.md) before filming.

| Show | Say |
| --- | --- |
| Open **Workflow**. On the **Apply for a California REAL ID card** task, show **Blocked**. Click **View checklist** and point to **Next steps**. | “Meet an international student at Cosumnes River College who wants to apply for a California REAL ID. The app shows that the task is blocked and points to the next step in this student’s workflow, which is gathering residency evidence.” |
| In **Task details**, show **Sources and dates** and the **Not verified** status next to a source. | “In Sources and dates, the student can see which sources still need review and open the official pages.” |
| Click **Finish first** to open the residency task, then click **Mark done**. Return to **Workflow** and show the REAL ID card with **Ready** and **Not verified** both visible. | “Now we’ll mark the residency step complete for our demo student. The REAL ID task moves to Ready because its prerequisite is complete. The Not verified label stays visible because the guidance still needs confirmation.” |

Keep the voice-over focused on the workflow and the visible source labels. Leave specific deadline, fee, document and eligibility claims out of this script and Devpost copy.

## Devpost description

International Student Navigator helps international students at Cosumnes River College see what to do next after arrival. In our demo, a fictional student opens a blocked California REAL ID task, sees which step comes first, and watches the workflow update when they mark that step complete. The dashboard links to official sources and shows when guidance still needs verification.
